Key Points Question Is use of amyloid positron emission tomography (PET) associated with subsequent change in the management of patients with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) or dementia of uncertain etiology? Findings In this longitudinal study that included 11 409 participants with MCI or dementia of uncertain cause, patient management 90 days after amyloid PET changed (compared with the pre-PET plan) in 60.2% of patients with MCI and 63.5% of patients with dementia. Meaning Amyloid PET was associated with changes in the subsequent management of diagnostically challenging patients with cognitive disorders.
